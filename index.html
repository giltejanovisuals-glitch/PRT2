04 — About Me

Designing visual experiences with creativity, clarity, and commercial purpose.

I’m Gil Tejano, a creative specialist and graphic designer with 6+ years of experience creating brand-focused visuals for furniture, lighting, retail, manufacturing, and events. My work spans campaign visuals, catalogs, showroom materials, sales presentations, and production-ready layouts.

I approach every project by understanding the brand, its audience, and the purpose behind each design — supporting product launches, maintaining brand consistency, and creating visual assets that strengthen brand presentation and customer engagement.

  • Brand-focused

    Visual systems that respect and strengthen established identities.

  • Multi-platform

    Experience across print, retail, showroom, digital, and event production.

  • Purpose-driven

    Design decisions guided by communication and commercial objectives.
